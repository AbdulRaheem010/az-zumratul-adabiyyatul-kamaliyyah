## Design reference
The visual direction was informed by the user-supplied screen recording of the Ali Institute website: white institutional header, cream content areas, warm brown sections, gold accents, centered section headings, rounded cards, process timeline, structured forms and image-led gallery. The Az-Zumratul site is an original design and does not copy the reference site's branding or content.

## Institutional research
Academic and institutional sources were used to inform conservative descriptions of the Adabiyya tradition and Az-Zumratul. Current school details such as leadership, official phone, programme specifics and admission rules remain configurable until confirmed by the madrasah.
