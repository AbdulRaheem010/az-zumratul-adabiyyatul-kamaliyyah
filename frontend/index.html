Academic journey

Four levels. One educational pathway.

The four class categories are central to the school's public-facing academic identity.

Tamheediyyah Preparatory Level

Foundation stage for building the essential habits, language skills and Islamic learning routines that support the next level.

01 · FOUNDATION
  • Foundational Arabic
  • Qur'anic learning
  • Adab & discipline

Ibtidā’iyyah Elementary Level

Structured elementary learning across Arabic and Islamic subjects, with steady development in reading, writing and religious knowledge.

02 · ELEMENTARY
  • Arabic language
  • Islamic studies
  • Qur'anic studies

Ihdādiyyah Intermediate Level

An intermediate stage for deeper study, stronger language proficiency and a more demanding academic routine.

03 · INTERMEDIATE
  • Arabic grammar
  • Fiqh & hadith
  • Literary studies

Thanawiyyah Advanced Level

Advanced secondary-stage studies designed to prepare learners for higher scholarship, further education and responsible community service.

04 · ADVANCED
  • Advanced Arabic
  • Classical texts
  • Higher Islamic studies
