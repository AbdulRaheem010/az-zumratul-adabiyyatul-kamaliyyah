Academics

Four levels. One clear pathway.

Academic categories are presented with Arabic terminology first and plain-language descriptions second.

Academic structure

The four categories of Zumratul.

Exact subjects, ages and duration should be set from the school's approved curriculum.

Tamheediyyah Preparatory

Foundation stage for early learners entering the madrasah's educational pathway.

LEVEL 01
  • Foundation Arabic
  • Qur'anic learning
  • Adab & routines

Ibtidā’iyyah Elementary

Elementary study with a balanced introduction to Arabic, Qur'anic and Islamic subjects.

LEVEL 02
  • Reading & writing
  • Islamic studies
  • Qur'an

Ihdādiyyah Intermediate

Intermediate studies with greater depth in language, religious sciences and classical learning.

LEVEL 03
  • Nahw & Sarf
  • Fiqh & Hadith
  • Arabic literature

Thanawiyyah Advanced

Advanced secondary studies intended to prepare students for higher education and scholarship.

LEVEL 04
  • Advanced Arabic
  • Classical texts
  • Higher Islamic studies
